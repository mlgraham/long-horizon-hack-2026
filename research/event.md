**Maximum team size four. One project per team. Build during the event.**

## The brief (verbatim from Luma)

> Long-horizon agents still break down over time: they accumulate observations, actions, and stale context
> until the growing history slows them down, costs more, and makes their own state less reliable.
> Fixing that means rethinking the architecture — explicit mutable state instead of ever-growing histories,
> agents that edit their own working context, and a clear split between what needs to persist and what can
> be discarded. This hackathon is about building that architecture.
